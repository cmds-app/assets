Release 26.5 · October 8

Compliance reporting in CMDS

The compliance summary now shows live data, and a new report shows the trend over time

CMDS End Users Meeting · Sep 24, 2026

Over the next five minutes I'll walk you through the changes to compliance reporting in CMDS. There are two parts to this. The Compliance Summary report now runs on live data, and a new Compliance Trend report shows how compliance has changed month by month. Both are available in our test environment now, and they'll be in the live environment on October 8. I'll leave some time for questions at the end.

Motivation

The compliance summary report is now up-to-the-minute

A popular request

Many administrators asked for live compliance results. On a typical day, CMDS records more than 1000 competency status changes and more than 600 achievement changes. Waiting until the next day to see that is not ideal.

Today

A nightly snapshot

The report reads a snapshot taken overnight. Depending on the time of day you run the report, the results are up to a day old.

In the next version (October 8)

Calculated live

The report is calculated from current data whenever you run the report. A competency status change made a minute ago is already in the numbers.

Result: the compliance score you see is the compliance score right now.

Until now, the Compliance Summary report hasn't read live data. It read from a snapshot that CMDS took every night. This means the report could be up to a day behind. On a typical day CMDS records more than 1000 competency status changes and more than 600 achievement changes, so everything in a busy day's work might not be available in the report until the next morning. Administrators asked us for numbers they could act on right away, so in this release the report is calculated from live data every time you run it. If someone validates a competency a minute before you run the report, it's in the results.

Here's the Compliance Summary report itself. If you use it today, very little changes on the screen. The criteria are the same: departments, membership, achievement types, and profiles. What's gone is the Report Type choice, the date range that went with it, and the banner telling you when the data was last refreshed, because there's no longer anything to refresh. It's also a lot faster. For the Orientation department at Keyera, which has about 1,700 people, the report used to take about 15 seconds on average. Now we have it running in less than 3 seconds, and that's while calculating everything live. The two history options, current and past compliance, and the past three months, have moved to a new report of their own, which is next.

In the Reports list

Right below the Compliance Summary

This is the new Compliance Trend report. You'll find it in the Reports list, right below the Compliance Summary. The criteria are short: choose your departments, up to 20, the same limit as the summary report. Choose the achievement types, and a period of either the past 12 months or the past 24 months. Then choose whether to count profiles that require compliance, or only primary profiles. The report shows one block for each department: a line chart of the compliance score, one point per month, with a table of the monthly scores underneath. If a department had no compliance requirements during the period, the report says so, rather than drawing an empty chart. The old history options compared a few dates side by side. This gives you up to two years at a glance, which makes it much easier to see whether a department is improving or slipping.

You can take the trend with you in two formats. Download XLSX gives you an Excel workbook with one worksheet for each department. Each worksheet has the monthly scores and its own line chart, so you can filter, add your own notes, or copy a chart straight into a presentation. You can download it from the criteria tab without running the report first, or from the report tab after. Download PDF gives you the report as you see it on screen, in landscape, with the charts included, which is handy for sending to someone who doesn't sign in to CMDS. As on the screen, a department with no requirements gets a short note rather than an empty chart, in both the workbook and the PDF.

Good to know

How the two reports differ

1

Live versus monthly

The summary is calculated live. The trend reads a snapshot taken each week, keeping the last one in each month.

2

Close, not identical

The snapshot counts department employment only, and primary or compliance profiles, so the latest month won't match the summary exactly.

3

Your departments only

Both reports list only the departments your account can access, exactly as the summary does today.

History is kept for 24 months

After October 8, CMDS keeps one department compliance snapshot per month for the past 24 months. Older snapshots are removed. If you need figures from before November 2024, export them before the release.

A few things worth knowing about how the two reports relate. First, the Compliance Summary is live, but the trend isn't, and can't be, because it's looking backwards. CMDS still takes a compliance snapshot every week, and the trend report reads the last snapshot in each month. Second, because of that, the two reports are close but not identical. The snapshot counts department employment only, not organization employment or data access, and it covers primary profiles and profiles that require compliance, not all profiles. So don't be surprised if the latest month on the trend is a point or two off the summary. The criteria page says this too. Third, both reports only list the departments your account can access, which is the same rule the summary uses today. If a department is missing from the list, check your access before assuming it's the report. And finally, the important one. From October 8, CMDS keeps one snapshot per month for the past 24 months, and removes the rest. Up to now it kept every weekly snapshot going back several years. If anyone relies on compliance figures from before November 2024, please export them before the release, and let us know if that's a problem.

Over to you

Questions and feedback

If time permits we can run both reports in the test environment

Likely questions, with short answers. Where did the Report Type option go? Current compliance is now the only type on the Compliance Summary. The two history types are replaced by the Compliance Trend report. Why does the trend not match the summary exactly? The trend reads monthly snapshots that count department employment only, while the summary is live and uses whatever membership and profile options you pick. Can the trend show all profiles? No. The snapshot only holds primary profiles and profiles that require compliance. Can it go back further than 24 months? No. From October 8, CMDS keeps 24 months of monthly snapshots and removes older ones. Why did the department count change? It used to count every membership record, including archived people and duplicates. It now counts the people the report actually shows. Is the summary still available as a download? Yes, the downloads work as before.
