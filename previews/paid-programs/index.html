Release 26.5 · October 8

Paid training programs in CMDS

Training programs with a fee can now be delivered in CMDS

CMDS End Users Meeting · Sep 24, 2026

Over the next six or seven minutes I'll show you how CMDS will handle training programs that have a fee. These new features are available in our test environment now, for anyone who's interested, and they'll be in the live environment on October 8. I'll leave some time for questions and feedback, and if there's time we can do a quick walkthrough in the system.

Motivation

Training programs with a fee can now be delivered in CMDS

What's driving it

Keyera is moving Validator Training online, with a fee. Training providers have also inquired about offering paid courses through the CMDS platform.

Today

A request has no next step

A learner can ask for a program, but nothing turns the request into access, and nothing records the fee.

in the Next version (October 8)

One workflow, from request to billing

The learner submits a request to enrol in the program, the right person approves it, and approval unlocks the training. Every enrolment request is kept for billing.

Result: paid training runs through CMDS, with a clear record of who approved it and who handles the billing.

Two things are driving this. Keyera is moving Validator Training sessions online, to help make the training more accessible, and allow for a lot more schedule flexibility. Training with an associated fee is something we've never delivered through CMDS before, so we're our own first customer for this new capability. We've also had training providers ask about offering paid courses through CMDS. CMDS can already host the training content and issue the credential, but in the past it hasn't been used to put a price on a program and control who has access to it. We've implemented a new workflow: anyone working in CMDS can browse the catalogue for paid programs, select one, and submit a request to enrol. That request goes to an administrator who approves it, and approval unlocks the training. The system keeps a record of every enrolment request for bookkeeping and billing.

Two ways to pay

Company-sponsored

Here's the entire workflow summarized in four steps. A learner searches the catalogue and finds the program, with its fee clearly visible. They open it, see exactly what they'll earn and the terms, and submit a request. The approver receives an email and approves or rejects the request. After it's approved, the program's achievements appear in the learner's training plan, and they can start. There are two ways to pay. Company-sponsored: the learner's own Company Administrators approve the request, based on their own business process. Self-funded: the learner pays CMDS directly, and CMDS administration approves the request after payment is received. Either way it's the same request and the same screens. The only difference is who decides and who pays.

For learners

Learners see the fee and what they'll earn

1  The fee shows on the catalogue card

2  What they'll earn, the terms, and two ways to pay

3  After submitting, the date and who to contact

This is what the learner sees. Paid programs are published in the catalogue with all the other elearning courses, with its fee in a blue tag at the top left. Clicking the program opens the program page - which shows a short summary, the achievements they'll earn with the required ones marked, the non-refundable terms, and two buttons, one for each way to pay. Each button asks them to confirm they agree to the terms for that option. After they click submit, the page shows a confirmation, with the date, and tells them who to contact. They can't send a second request for the same program while the first is open, so approvers don't need to worry about getting duplicate requests for the same program from the same person.

No Company Administrators? CMDS administration decides

Learners kept informed

An email when a request is approved or rejected

Here's a quick look at the approver's side of the workflow. For a company-sponsored request, an email message goes to the learner's Company Administrators. The email includes the learner's name, the program, how it's being paid for, and the fee, and a link to open the request. From there it's one click to approve or reject. A few things to note: Each company only ever sees its own requests. If a company has no Company Administrators set up, then the request goes to CMDS administration instead, so nothing gets lost or stuck. Self-funded requests always go to CMDS administration. Either way, the learner receives an email when the request is approved or rejected. If a request is rejected, nothing is assigned, and the learner can send a new request later.

After approval

Approved learners use the plan they know

1  The Program filter narrows the plan to one program

2  Home page: the catalogue button and My Programs

Certificates for course completion are issued when every achievement is earned, as it is today.

After a request is approved, there's nothing new in the system. The program's achievements are added to the learner's training plan, which is the same screen everyone already uses. A new Program filter at the top allows them to filter to the program that's been approved for them. On the home page, a My Programs tile lists the learner's programs and opens the plan directly, and a new button opens the program catalogue. When the learner has earned every achievement in the program, the program completion certificate is issued, just as it is today, if you're using program-level certificates.

3

4

Direct enrolment

An administrator enrolling someone must choose who pays

5

No group shortcuts

Paid programs can't be assigned to a whole group

Every request is kept as a record for billing purposes. The fee is stored when the learner submits their request, so even if the price for a program changes later, they pay what they saw. Along with that, every program enrolment request stores the learner's name and email address, who approved or rejected the request, and when that decision was made. The record stays in the database even if the learner leaves and their account is archived. If an administrator enrols someone directly, CMDS asks who is paying first, so those enrolments can be billed too. And paid programs can't be assigned to a whole group at once, which means nobody gets into a paid program without a billing record.

Over to you

Questions and feedback

If time permits we can look at the catalogue in the test environment

Likely questions, with short answers. Can learners pay online? Not in this release. Self-funded learners pay CMDS directly, and CMDS approves once the payment is in. What about programs without a fee? Administrators enrol learners in them exactly as before. If a free program is placed in a catalogue, learners request it the same way, just without a price. Can we change the email wording? Yes. A company can have its own wording for each of the three emails; otherwise the standard CMDS wording is used. Who approves if our Company Administrator is away or leaves? The email goes to everyone on the Company Administrators team when the learner asks, so keep the team current. With nobody on it, CMDS administration decides. How does an approver see every pending request? There's a search page with all of their company's pending requests, and approve and reject on each. What happens after a rejection? The learner gets an email, nothing is assigned, and they can send a new request.
